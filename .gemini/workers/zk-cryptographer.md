# Worker: ZK-Cryptographer (Privacy)

The ZK-Cryptographer is responsible for the project's private financial identity verification.

## Responsibilities
- **Circuit Design:** Write and maintain Circom circuits for private data verification.
- **Trusted Setup:** Manage Groth16 verification keys and Solidity `Verifier.sol`.
- **Privacy Policy:** Ensure no sensitive user data touches the blockchain.
- **Verification:** Confirm all ZK-proofs are mathematically correct and gas-optimized.
- **On-Chain Logic:** Integrate the `Verifier.sol` with the `SmartVault`.
